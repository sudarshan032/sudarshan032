Measurement · Freedom With AI

Did the AI calling channel actually cause revenue?

A causal study across 100+ webinars and five audience cohorts. It separates incremental revenue from influenced revenue and corrects for selection confounds instead of reporting raw uplift. One headline ROI figure turned out to be correlation; the call transcripts showed those calls had been attendance reminders, not sales.

Finding: AI calls lifted purchases far more than attendance (+20–70% vs +4–5%). Conversations beyond three turns predicted a 7.8% purchase rate, while call connection alone predicted nothing. Engagement, not connection, was the lever. The method became a reusable reporting SOP.
Python · PostgreSQL · Causal inference

Match Check

A deal-breaker detector for matchmakers. Claude pulls deal-breakers out of free-text bios, returning either an exact quote or null. A deterministic engine then scores the match (multiplicative deal-breakers, weighted fit, geometric mean of both sides), so every verdict can be traced.

The LLM reads, the rules decide. No verdict depends on the model's judgement alone.

Published & in progress

IEEE IGARSS 2025
First author

Obstacle Avoidance in Drones Using Deep Reinforcement Learning

DQL and DDPG agents trained in Unity3D compare 2D LiDAR against depth estimation for fixed-wing UAV obstacle avoidance. LiDAR won for real-time control, and the paper proposes hybrid sensing for production.

With Ravi Boda, Subhranginee Das, K. Arun Kumar, Mousmi Ajay Chaurasia · DOI 10.1109/IGARSS55030.2025.11243721
Independent
2026

NeuroWrite: human-like handwriting generation

A diffusion model of structured intra-writer variation. Nobody writes the same letter twice, and the variation follows a pattern. Writer style is modelled as a distribution N(μ, Σ), with a neuromotor-inspired noise schedule and a repetition-memory cross-attention module. It also proposes a Writer Variation Fidelity metric (1 − Wasserstein-1 over pairwise distance distributions).

PyTorch · Apple MLX · trained on a pool of 3 Kaggle + 3 Lightning AI free-tier accounts
